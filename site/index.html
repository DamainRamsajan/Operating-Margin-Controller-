What it does

Certified model changes

A signed pass, hold or reject verdict on a replacement model or vendor, usually from a small number of labeled disagreements.

Billing audit

An independent estimate of your true resolution rate, compared with the resolutions you are billed for, under the vendor's definition and yours.

Action authority audit

How much money your AI moved through refunds and credits outside policy, with confidence intervals.

Human-access evidence

Measured disclosure and time-to-human rates for the new chatbot rules, with signed statements. We measure; your counsel interprets.

Why you can trust the answer

Every result is a signed statement. The verifier is open source and small enough to read, so your auditor can check a result without trusting us. We do not sell AI agents or models, and we never charge fees that depend on what we find.

Verifier and format: omc-verify on GitHub and PyPI (see the link below once published).

Early results

On 13,083 real public banking-support queries, with two classifier versions and a known true change in error rate, the audit certified equivalent and improved versions and never certified a worse one. It is not yet validated on live traffic, on LLM agents, or on your data. That is what the pilot is for.
