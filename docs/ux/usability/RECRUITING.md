# Recruiting

## Who
| Persona | Screener (must meet all) | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Support operations lead | Manages or advises a support team of 20 or more; their team uses an AI agent or chatbot; they help decide model or vendor changes | 5 |
| Finance controller or analyst | Reviews vendor invoices or technology spend of at least six figures a year; has dealt with a usage- or outcome-based vendor | 5 |
| Expert reviewer | Has done quality review or labeling of support conversations | 3 to 5 |
| Auditor or compliance | Has reviewed technology controls or compliance evidence | 3 to 5 |
| Developer | Maintains prompts or model configuration for an AI feature and uses pull requests | 3 to 5 |

Exclude: anyone who works for a direct competitor, and close friends who will not criticize.

## Where to find them
Your own network and its introductions first; LinkedIn posts and direct messages to people in the roles above; support-leader and CX communities; the design-partner conversations from the launch plan (a usability session is an easy first ask). Use the Google Calendar connector (once connected) to propose slots.

## Outreach message (plain prose, edit the brackets)
Hi [Name], I am building a tool that shows support and finance teams, in plain language, whether an AI model or vendor change is safe. I would like 45 minutes of your time to watch you use a prototype and tell me honestly where it is confusing. No customer data is involved: everything on screen is demo data. I can share the findings with you afterwards. Would [two time options] work?

## Screener questions (ask by message or at the start of the call)
1. What is your role, and how many people are on your team?
2. Does your team use an AI chatbot or agent for customer support? Which tool?
3. Who decides when the model or vendor changes?
4. Do you review vendor invoices or usage charges?

## Incentives
A small gift card or a summary of findings and early access. Do not offer anything that depends on their feedback being positive.

## Ethics and consent
Tell participants before they agree: purpose, that sessions may be recorded, how long recordings are kept, that they can stop at any time, and that demo data only is shown. Store recordings and notes under participant codes (P01, P02), not names. `CONSENT.md` has a template.
