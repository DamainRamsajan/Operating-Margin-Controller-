# ADR-030: Channel strategy

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Context:** Salesforce unified its marketplace in April 2026, ServiceNow launched a build partner program in January 2026, and Microsoft ties co-sell to committed cloud spend; the same companies are building competing control planes [S41][V].
- **Decision:** List on marketplaces matched to the design partner's helpdesk or cloud commitment to shorten procurement; sell entry products directly; confirm fee schedules before modeling; apply ADR-016 to any co-sell or revenue-share incentive.
- **Consequences:** Faster procurement and distribution; fee pressure and bundling risk (R34); marketplace terms may constrain data handling.
