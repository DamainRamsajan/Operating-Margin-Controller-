# ADR-026: Packaging and pricing principles

- **Status:** Accepted (26 October 2026, founder decision D7)
- **Context:** Compliance-automation analogues price by size, frameworks and modules and leave audits to partners; contingent fees would break independence (ADR-016) [S34][V].
- **Decision:** Four tiers (Qualify, Audit, Control, Comply); fixed fees for entry products; subscription by workflow and module after embedding; partner-delivered attestations priced by partners; no fee, referral fee or revenue share may depend on audit findings or on a specific vendor's results; relationships disclosed; shadow-scoring inference passed through with a cap.
- **Consequences:** Predictable, defensible revenue; slower than contingency pricing; prices set with design partners (D6).
