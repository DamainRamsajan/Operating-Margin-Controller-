# ADR-017: Beachhead is customer-support ticket resolution

- **Status:** Accepted (23 October 2026, decided by delegation)
- **Context:** Candidates scored on volume, label speed, AI already deployed with vendor or model churn, outcome-priced vendors, regulatory exposure, data access and sales cycle. Support ticket resolution scores highest: per-resolution pricing is mainstream, resolution and reopen signals are fast labels, many deployments depend on retiring models, and Annex III exposure is lower than claims or KYC if worker monitoring is avoided [S27][S30][V]. Scores for IT service desk, payables exceptions, claims and KYC rest on earlier rounds and general knowledge, with lower confidence.
- **Decision:** First workflow family is AI-handled and AI-assisted support tickets (chat and email). IT service desk is second. Payables exceptions and regulated workflows follow.
- **Consequences:** Connector priorities follow helpdesk and AI-vendor exports; QA tools become partners; avoid any per-agent scoring. Reassess after the first design partner.
