# ADR-016: Independence charter

- **Status:** Accepted (23 October 2026, founder decision D3)
- **Context:** Every suite and lab sells agents or models and cannot credibly audit its own value claims.
- **Decision:** OMC does not sell agents or models; accepts no payment contingent on a specific vendor's results or on audit findings; discloses commercial relationships; shows vendor and customer outcome definitions side by side; and lets customers re-run any statement without OMC services.
- **Consequences:** Constrains partnerships and pricing; becomes a buying criterion and a structural moat against bundlers.
