# ADR-020: Outcome billing audit and vendor bake-off

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Context:** Outcome-priced vendors define the billed outcome; one leading vendor counts silence after an answer as a resolution, vendor-claimed resolution rates exceed independent test results, and no standard commercial unit exists [S27][V].
- **Decision:** Provide an independent true-outcome estimator with intervals (prediction-powered), billing reconciliation under vendor and customer definitions, dispute evidence packs, and randomized propensity-logged vendor bake-offs under the risk gate. Fees are fixed; none depends on findings.
- **Consequences:** Direct dollar value and recurring use as outcome-priced spend grows; may strain vendor relationships (R25); needs export coverage (spike S-e) and vendor data terms (R22).
