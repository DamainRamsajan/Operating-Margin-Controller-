# ADR-024: Customer-labeled gold and the Review Workbench

- **Status:** Accepted (26 October 2026, founder decision D7)
- **Context:** Label labor is the main variable cost of audits and would otherwise become cost of goods; independence is easier to defend when the customer's own experts produce gold labels.
- **Decision:** OMC provides sampling plans, blinded comparison, time-boxed budgets and label provenance in a Review Workbench; customer experts and partner QA tools supply labels; OMC employs no labeling staff.
- **Consequences:** Low cost of goods and clear independence; depends on customer expert availability (R29); labeling quality must be monitored through audits of the labels themselves.
