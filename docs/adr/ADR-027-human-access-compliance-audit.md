# ADR-027: Human access and disclosure compliance audit

- **Status:** Proposed (pending spike S-j)
- **Context:** California AB 1609 (signed 28 September 2026, applies from 1 January 2027 to large businesses), Spain Law 10/2025 (compliance deadline 28 December 2026) and EU AI Act Article 50 (since 2 August 2026) make disclosure and timely access to a human measurable obligations; committee analysis calls the failure mode chatbot purgatory [S37][V]. Much of the measurement is deterministic (timestamps) and the rest is sampled review.
- **Decision:** Add BB22 as a Comply-tier module: rule sets as versioned data entered and confirmed by counsel, deterministic timing checks, sampled expert review with prediction-powered correction, rates with intervals by channel and jurisdiction, and signed Compliance Statements that report measurements and make no legal conclusions.
- **Consequences:** Near-term deadlines create a sales trigger; legal interpretation risk (R31) is managed by counsel-owned rule sets; scope creep risk (R35) is managed by limiting the module to measurement.
