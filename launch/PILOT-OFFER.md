# Pilot offer (30 days, read-only)

**What the partner gets:** a model-exposure report; a certified pass, hold or reject verdict on one model or vendor change, as a signed statement their auditors can verify offline; a first estimate of true resolution rate versus billed resolutions (if a billing export is available); a short methodology readout.

**What we need:** a pseudonymized export (case id, timestamp, decision per model version, human labels for a sample), 2 to 4 hours from one analyst and a few hours from reviewers for labels. No message text, names or emails. Policy: `docs/dev/DATA-REQUEST.md`.

**What we do not do:** write to any system, store raw data outside the partner's environment, or charge fees that depend on findings (arc42 ADR-016).

**Success criteria (agreed up front):** a signed verdict is produced from the partner's data; label count per verdict is within the agreed budget; the partner's finance or audit contact can verify a statement without our help.

**Commercial terms.** First two partners: free under the Common Paper Design Partner Agreement, in exchange for feedback and permission to publish an anonymized result. After that: the Common Paper Pilot Agreement with a fixed fee. **Price is a hypothesis to test in the first five calls: ask what the problem is worth before quoting; my untested guess is low five figures for a 30-day paid pilot.**

**Data handling summary.** See `SECURITY-PACK.md`.
